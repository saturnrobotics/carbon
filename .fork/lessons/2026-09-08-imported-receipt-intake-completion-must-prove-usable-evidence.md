## Receipt intake completion must prove usable evidence

**Context:** Connecting payment imports, attachment collection, extraction, review, and approval.

**Problem:** Aggregate registration and completed worker counts concealed metadata-only previews, missing payment context, and unprocessed ambiguous documents. Synthetic upload tests did not exercise the imported-payment source shape.

**Rule:** Verify the entire source-to-review path with payment metadata and actual eligible file references. Report collection, extraction, readiness, and approval separately. Preview only real files; preserve acquisition failures explicitly; never claim source accuracy from a completed job alone.

**Applies to:** Payment integrations, document intake, review interfaces, and deployment acceptance.
